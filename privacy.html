Privacy Policy

ToolVerse Uploader is used solely by the owner of the ToolVerse YouTube channel. It accesses the owner's YouTube account through the YouTube API Services only to upload videos, set metadata, thumbnails, captions and publishing schedules, and to read the channel's own video statistics.

No data from any other user is collected. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

This tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
